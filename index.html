Questions

No. You create a separate user for me (WordPress admin, Shopify collaborator, or a DNS user) and remove it when we're done.

What if my problem isn't listed?

Send it anyway. If it's something I can fix, I'll give you a price. If not, I'll tell you and point you in the right direction.

How fast is delivery?

Most small fixes are delivered in 1 to 3 days. I confirm the time before we start.

Can you guarantee my emails won't go to spam?

No one can promise 100% inbox placement, but correct SMTP and DNS setup improves delivery a lot. I'll show you test results.
